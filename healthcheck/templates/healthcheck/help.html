Help Centre

This page explains how to use the SKY Engineering web application, including Teams, Organisation, Messages, Schedule, and Reports.

Teams

Search teams, view managers, see team members, dependencies, skills, and contact details.

Messages

Send new messages, view inbox items, check sent messages, and save drafts.

Schedule

Create meeting schedules, choose a platform, and manage upcoming meetings.

Organisation

Explore departments, team types, and how teams relate to each other.

Reports

Generate PDF or Excel reports such as number of teams and teams without managers.

Getting Started

  • Open the Team page to search for engineering teams and review their details.
  • Use Organisation to understand departments, team types, and dependencies.
  • Go to Messages to send team updates, save drafts, or check inbox and sent items.
  • Use Schedule to create and manage meetings for teams.
  • Check Reports to generate summaries and exports.

Common Problems

  • If you cannot find a team, try using fewer search terms.
  • If a message fails, make sure the recipient, subject, and message body are filled in.
  • If a meeting is not saved, check the date, time, and platform fields.
  • If a page looks incorrect, refresh the browser and try again.
