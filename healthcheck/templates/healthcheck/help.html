Help Center

How to Schedule a Meeting

Navigate to the Schedule page, select your desired date and time, choose the appropriate team, and click "Schedule Meeting". Make sure the date is not in the past.

Viewing Reports

Go to the Reports tab to view interactive charts regarding team distribution. You can also export a CSV spreadsheet of the raw data from there.
